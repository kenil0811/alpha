## Usable generated screens

**Daily Food Log** (calorie):
- The render check passed 52 of 52 checks on the first attempt. They cover the empty state,
  the primary interaction saving a record, populated data including a very long food name, a
  failed read shown as an error, a failed save, and no sideways scrolling at 768 px.
- In use, the screen's own labels were enough:
  - Food, Amount, and Calories ("Leave it blank and Alpha estimates it for you");
  - meal buttons and Add entry;
  - Today, with a total and the day's entries marked "(estimate)" where Alpha estimated;
  - earlier and later days;
  - 7- and 30-day trends.
- Three meals were logged, with 0 script errors, 0 blocked requests, and scroll width equal to
  client width at 1280 and 768 px.

**Job Opening Tracker** (collection/review):
- The first attempt's screen did not compile: it passed a state setter where a change handler
  belongs. The trusted build's type check caught this, and the repair passed 49 of 49 checks.
- In use:
  - add an opening;
  - stage filters with counts;
  - Open shows a drawer with the opening's details, "Log a step" (date, what I did, note),
    "Change the stage" and History.
- Two openings, a logged step and a stage change were saved, with 0 script errors, 0 blocked
  requests and no sideways scrolling.

**Fits In Today Planner** (held-out) has **no custom dashboard**. The brief's surfaces are
`conversation` only, the Version has no `ui` and no `dist/ui`, and its three actions are run from
Alpha's actions view:
- one form per action, built from the input schema;
- results in plain words;
- a Stop button for long runs.

Its outputs are what the person asked for:
- the plan in pasted order with running totals;
- a "won't fit" list;
- estimated times marked `estimated`.

The shell's actions view is covered by `apps/desktop/src/workflows/workflows.test.tsx`: the form
built from the schema, the origin rule, refusal messages, Stop, and saved data with "(estimate)".

## Found and reported, not hidden

- **Kit trend chart.** It draws very large axis labels and bars when only one day has data
  (`screens/calorie-use-three-meals.png`). This is a shared kit issue, not the App's.
- **Required fields.** The kit does not stop an empty submission on the screen. A click on
  "Add opening" with nothing typed reached the App, and the App refused it as a
  `handler_exception`.
- **Refusal wording.** The held-out App's refusal reads "ValueError: Paste your to-do list, one
  item per line, and run it again." The platform passes the exception's class name through to
  the person.
- **Not verified by me in the native window.** Generated screens are served by the host at
  `alpha-ui://<app>/<version>/index.html`. That route compiles, and it serves the same sealed
  files used here, but I have not seen it rendered in the Alpha window. The attempt was cut
  short: both Alpha apps on the Mac were quit a minute into it, and I did not retry without
  asking. Opening a G1 App in the Alpha window is the first step of the M1 review.
