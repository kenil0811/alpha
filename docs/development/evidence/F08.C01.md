  shell's bridge host and grant, against the real Core. Three meals were logged:
  - "2 rotis and dal", 1 plate, Lunch: estimated at 370;
  - "masala chai", 1 cup, 90 typed by the person, Snack;
  - "paneer butter masala with jeera rice", 1 bowl, Dinner: estimated at 600.

  The saved records carry `calories_are_estimate` true, false and true. The screen shows
  "(estimate)" beside estimated values and a total of 1,060 kcal, and "Last 30 days" works.
  There were no script errors and no sideways scrolling at 1280 or 768 px.
- **Job tracker, through its own screen.**
  - Two openings were added: Zenith Foods, Supply Chain Planner, from LinkedIn, with notes;
    and Harbor Health, Operations Analyst, from a referral.
  - Harbor Health was opened. "Applied" was logged with the note "Sent CV and cover letter",
    and the stage was changed to Applied.
  - Storage holds 2 openings and 2 step records, and the filter reads "Applied (1)".
  - The first use run stopped at a driver mistake: the loose name "Open" matched "Add opening"
    and submitted an empty form, which the App refused. That run is kept as `use-1.json`.
- **Held-out, through its actions** (as the shell's actions view runs them, with origin
  `user`):
  - `fit_plan` on six pasted items with 4 hours planned 215 of 240 minutes in pasted order.
  - Groceries (60 min) and the email clean-out (90 min) were listed as "won't fit".
  - "Book dentist appointment" had no time given and got a 30-minute estimate, marked

## Failed G1 attempts (kept, not relabelled)

1. **Freeze 518d187.** The planner's schema carried Pydantic's `discriminator`, which the CLI's
   strict schema check refuses. The creation stopped at planning. Fixed in f2cb237.
2. **Freeze f2cb237.** The builder used its whole 12-minute attempt on a large brief, and the
   build ended with its remaining budget unused. Fixed in 45f962c: a timed-out attempt is now
   continued within the same limits, the builder is told its time and to keep code small, and
   first versions stay small.
3. **Freeze 45f962c.** The plan expected `"*"` to mean "any value"; the matcher compared it
   literally, so every behaviour check failed. The run was stopped before a repair could game
   the check. Fixed in f7dada6 with `{"$any": true}`.

In every case the fix was to platform code, followed by a new freeze and a fresh G1 run from the
first request. Generated source was never edited.
