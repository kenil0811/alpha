## F09: the trend chart

`TrendChart` is now drawn with CSS at a fixed pixel height (160 px, clamped to 80–240), with
fixed 0.75 rem lettering. Bars are at most 48 px wide, and the plot is only as wide as its bars.
Gaps stay dashed "no entry" slots, a recorded zero is a baseline line, and the legend gives
coverage ("Days with entries: 1 of 7"). The accessible summary and the numbers table are kept.

**Qualification in the pinned browser** (`tests/ui/test_kit_trend.py`) runs the real kit build
and a gallery on the reference page (`?charts=1`):
- cases: 1 day, 7 days with one logged, 7 with none, 7 with a recorded zero, 30 days with gaps,
  and a long title;
- widths: 1002 px and 670 px, the width an App's screen gets inside Alpha at the default and at
  the minimum window.

| | 1002 px | 670 px |
|---|---|---|
| Plot height, every case | 160 px | 160 px |
| Largest lettering | 12 px | 12 px |
| Bars overflow / page scrolls sideways | no / no | no / no |

**The old chart fails the same test**: a one-day chart at 670 px measured 2,488 px tall, with
171 px lettering.

## Required fields, focus and drafts (kit `Form`)

- `Form` checks required fields itself, because the sandboxed frame forbids the browser's
  validation. An empty required field stops the submission. The form says "Fill in Company
  first.", marks the field invalid and focuses it; the message clears as the person types.
  (Found in G1: an empty "Add opening" reached the App.)
- After a save that cleared the fields, focus returns to the first field, for the next entry.
- A failed save keeps what was typed.
- Kit tests: 3 new, 58 in total.

## Plain-language refusals

Core now words a handler's exception for people, where the worker's error is recorded, for live
runs and previews alike:
- a `ValueError` or `InvalidValue` becomes the App's own message ("Amount is needed.");
- anything else becomes "Something went wrong in this workflow.";
- the class name and text stay in `technical` for Activity details.

Activity shows "Not done: …" with the App's message, instead of "Reason: handler_exception".
Unit test with the G1 refusals.

## The render check: composition, repeat entry and drafts

`workers/validator/src/ui_check.mjs` now requires:
- **`ui.primary.visible`**: the main interaction's first control is visible without scrolling in
  1002 × 480, the App screen's size in Alpha's default window;
- **`ui.primary.repeat`**: a second entry made straight after the first is saved without
  reloading, or the screen says plainly why not;
- **`ui.error.save_input_kept`**: previously advisory, now required.

On a real fixture build all three ran and passed. Two new fixture variants fail the right check:
- `ui_entry_below`: a tall introduction pushes the entry off-screen, and `ui.primary.visible`
  fails;
- `ui_clears_on_failure`: a hand-made form empties the field before the save is known, and
  `ui.error.save_input_kept` fails.

The kit reference (`REFERENCE.md`), which the builder reads, now says how to lay out a screen
around the main interaction and what the check requires.
